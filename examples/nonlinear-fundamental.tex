\par\smallskip
\small Illustrative isotropic B--H table; no magnetic loss or resolved eddy
currents. The approximation retains the fundamental of $H(\widehat B\cos\omega t)$.
Higher harmonics, waveform distortion and hysteresis are excluded. Coil currents
are prescribed and in phase. Frequency only sets the induced terminal voltage.
